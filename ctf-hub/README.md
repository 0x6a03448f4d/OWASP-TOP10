# CTF-Style Challenges Hub 🏆

Welcome to the OWASP Top 10 CTF Challenges Hub! This unified platform allows you to track your progress, earn badges, compete on leaderboards, and earn certificates as you master security vulnerabilities.

## 🎯 Features

- **Unified Lab Launcher**: Launch all labs from one central interface
- **Progress Tracking**: Monitor your completion across all OWASP categories
- **Achievement Badges**: Earn badges as you complete challenges
- **Leaderboard**: Compete with others (local tracking)
- **Completion Certificates**: Auto-generated PDF certificates
- **Knowledge Assessment**: Track your learning progress

## 🚀 Getting Started

1. Open `index.html` in your web browser
2. Browse available challenges across OWASP categories
3. Launch labs directly from the interface
4. Mark challenges as complete when finished
5. Track your progress and earn badges!

## 📊 Progress Tracking

Your progress is automatically saved in your browser's local storage:
- Total challenges completed
- Category-wise completion
- Time spent on challenges
- Badges earned
- Achievement milestones

## 🏅 Badge System

Earn badges as you progress:

### Beginner Badges
- **First Steps**: Complete your first challenge
- **Web Warrior**: Complete all Web Top 10
- **API Expert**: Complete all API Top 10
- **LLM Guardian**: Complete all LLM Top 10
- **Mobile Master**: Complete all Mobile Top 10

### Advanced Badges
- **Security Scholar**: Complete 25 challenges
- **Vulnerability Hunter**: Complete 40 challenges
- **OWASP Champion**: Complete all 40 challenges
- **Speed Demon**: Complete 10 challenges in one day
- **Perfectionist**: Score 100% on all quizzes

## 🏆 Leaderboard

The leaderboard tracks:
- Total challenges completed
- Completion percentage
- Average quiz scores
- Recent activity
- Achievement count

**Note**: Leaderboard is stored locally. For team competitions, you can export/import progress data.

## 📜 Certificates

Earn printable PDF certificates when you:
- Complete all challenges in a category
- Complete all 40 challenges (Master Certificate)
- Achieve perfect quiz scores

Certificates include:
- Your name
- Completion date
- Category/achievement earned
- Unique certificate ID
- QR code for verification
