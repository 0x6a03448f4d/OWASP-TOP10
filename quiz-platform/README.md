## 📊 Features

- ✅ **Instant Feedback**: See correct answers immediately
- ✅ **Score Tracking**: Monitor your progress over time
- ✅ **Detailed Explanations**: Learn why answers are correct/incorrect
- ✅ **Timed Mode**: Practice under exam conditions
- ✅ **Randomized Questions**: Different quiz every time
- ✅ **Knowledge Retention**: Spaced repetition reminders
- ✅ **Mobile-Friendly**: Take quizzes anywhere

## 🎓 How It Works

1. **Choose a quiz** based on your learning goals
2. **Answer questions** - multiple choice, true/false, scenario-based
3. **Get instant feedback** with detailed explanations
4. **Review mistakes** and learn from them
5. **Track progress** in your profile

## 📈 Knowledge Retention Tracker

The platform tracks:
- Quiz scores over time
- Topics you've mastered
- Areas needing improvement
- Recommended review schedule
- Learning streaks

## 🏆 Scoring System

- **90-100%**: Excellent - You've mastered this topic! 🏆
- **75-89%**: Good - Solid understanding, minor gaps 👍
- **60-74%**: Fair - Review recommended 📚
- **Below 60%**: Needs Work - Study more and retake 💪
